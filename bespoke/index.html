Bespoke / Custom

Built to your wall, not to a standard.

Most of what leaves our workshop was never in a catalogue. Bring a photograph, a drawing, or just a measurement of the space — we take it from there.

How a commission works

  1. The brief

    A conversation on WhatsApp or in the showroom. What the piece is for, where it goes, what it must hold, what you like and hate. Photographs of the space help enormously.

  2. The drawing

    We draw the piece to your dimensions — plan, elevation and the carving detail. You mark it up until it is right. Nothing is cut before you approve the drawing.

  3. The quote

    A fixed price against the approved drawing, with the timber, finish and upholstery specified in writing. No surprises at delivery.

  4. The build

    Seasoned hardwood, pegged mortise-and-tenon joinery, hand carving and hand finishing. Typical lead time is four to twelve weeks depending on the carving. We send progress photographs.

  5. Delivery

    We deliver and place the piece ourselves within Chattogram, and arrange careful transport beyond it. You check it against the drawing before we leave.
