What a visit looks like

  1. Walk the floor

    Catalogue pieces are out and sittable. Open the drawers, lean on the arms — that's what they're there for.

  2. Handle the materials

    Timber samples, velvet and leather swatches, gilding samples. Take swatches home against your curtains.

  3. Talk to the maker

    The workshop is behind the showroom. If you're commissioning, you can see work at your stage of build and judge the standard directly.
