Why choose Heaven

What you can count on.

  1. Made to order, not off the shelf

    Nothing is cut until you commission it. Lead times run from two to twelve weeks depending on the piece, and every piece page states its own.

  2. Built to your dimensions where it counts

    Beds, dining tables and bespoke cabinetry are made to the size you give us. On everything else the published size is the size we build — we would rather say so than offer a slider that changes nothing.

  3. You choose the timber, finish and fabric

    Mahogany, teak, walnut and beech. Velvet, damask, bouclé and full-grain leather. Every option is listed on the materials page and priced as you pick it.

  4. See it in your room before you commit

    8 of the 18 pieces carry a 3D model you can stand on your own floor at actual size, from your phone. No app to install.

  5. A price before you ask for one

    Every piece publishes a range, and the configurator totals your exact specification — material, size, add-ons and delivery — before you send anything.

  6. Delivery and installation inside Chattogram, included

    Anywhere in the city, our own people deliver and install at no extra cost. Outside the city it is a flat ৳4,500, and you see it in the total.

  7. No account, no card details, no online payment

    You confirm your order on WhatsApp or by email and settle at the showroom or on delivery. This site never asks you to create an account or type a card number into it — that is a deliberate choice, not a missing feature.
