The workshop

The joint is the detail.

Heaven Furniture Mart has been building furniture in Chattogram since 2020. Not assembling it — building it: sawn hardwood, pegged joints, carving done with gouges rather than routers, finishes rubbed in by hand.

Philosophy

Structure over effects.

Anyone can spray a sheen. What can't be faked is a pegged mortise-and-tenon joint — so where a frame is exposed, that is deliberate. You are meant to see how the piece holds together, because how it holds together is what you are paying for.

Every piece is made to order. That isn't a luxury affectation: it means the bed fits your mattress, the showcase fits your alcove, and the sofa fits through your stairwell — because we asked before we cut.

The hands

Carvers, joiners, finishers, upholsterers.

The workshop sits behind the showroom on Agrabad Access Road. Carving is done in solid hardwood — no applied resin ornament. Gilding is water-gilded in place. Nailhead trim is set by hand, one stud at a time, because a machine-set row never sits quite straight on a carved rail.

If you commission a piece, you're welcome to visit mid-build and watch your own frame come together.
